Federal Identity & Security

Libraries for DoD and federal agency identity management — CAC/PIV authentication, PKI certificate handling, and HSPD-12 compliance. Built on real-world deployment experience.

pki-core

Generic X.509 certificate utilities for PKI-enabled applications. Certificate parsing, CRL caching with stale-while-revalidate refresh, pluggable identity extraction with provider registry, trust store management, and a composable validation pipeline.
